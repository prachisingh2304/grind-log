Daily knee, calf & hamstring care

Do this before training, and on rest days on its own. ~8–10 minutes. Since you've had a knee muscle injury with pain after sitting cross-legged a while, and tightness in your calves and hamstrings, the aim here is gentle mobility plus building strength around the knee — not pushing through pain.

Stop any movement that causes sharp or increasing pain around the knee. Avoid holding deep knee bends (e.g. long deep squats, sitting back on your heels, kneeling on hard floors) until you've been pain-free for a while. If pain around the knee is ongoing or getting worse rather than easing with this kind of gentle work, it's worth getting it looked at by a physio — they can check what's actually going on with the muscle and give you something targeted, which I can't diagnose from here.
